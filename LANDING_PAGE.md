## 5. Mechanism Section: The Process

- **Headline:** The Fast Solutions Method: Problem First, Code Last
- **Subheadline:** Most projects fail because someone started building before anyone understood the problem. We do it in the opposite order.

### Step 1: Find the Problem
*What's actually costing you?*
We audit your site, your lead flow and your competitors to find exactly where customers and hours are leaking.
**You get:** a site audit, a funnel and conversion analysis, a competitor scan and a prioritised problem list.

### Step 2: Research & Plan
*What's the smartest fix?*
We build a clear plan: what to fix, what to build, in what order and why.
**You get:** a solution roadmap, a tech recommendation, a timeline and milestones, and a fixed price.

### Step 3: Design First
*Does this look and feel right?*
You see and approve the full design (mobile and desktop) before any code is written. No surprises.
**You get:** wireframes, user flows and high-fidelity mockups.

### Step 4: Build
*Is it built to last?*
Your approved design becomes fast, clean, documented production code, with automations and custom tools wired in.
**You get:** a production-ready build, performance tuning, an SEO foundation and CMS/backend integration.

### Step 5: Test & Launch
*Is everything working?*
We test on every device, check speed and accessibility, launch without downtime and hand everything over.
**You get:** a QA report, a performance audit, go-live support and handover docs.

- **Why it works:**
  - **Diagnosis before prescription.** You pay to fix the real problem, not the one someone guessed at.
  - **Design approval before code.** Changes cost minutes, not weeks.
  - **One team, end to end.** No handoffs between a designer, a developer and an "automation guy."

- **Your involvement (3 touchpoints only):**
  1. Confirm the problem
  2. Approve the design
  3. Sign off before launch
